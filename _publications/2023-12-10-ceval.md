This paper introduces C-Eval, a comprehensive multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite designed to assess the knowledge and capabilities of foundation models in Chinese. The benchmark covers a wide range of subjects and difficulty levels, providing a robust evaluation framework for Chinese-language models. Authors include Yuzhen Huang, Yuzhuo Bai, Zhihao Zhu, Junlei Zhang, Jinghan Zhang, Tangjun Su, Junteng Liu, Chuancheng Lv, Yikai Zhang, Jiayi Lei, Yao Fu, Maosong Sun, and Junxian He.
