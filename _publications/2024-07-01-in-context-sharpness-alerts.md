This paper proposes using in-context sharpness as alerts for hallucination mitigation, approaching the problem from an inner representation perspective. The work investigates how the sharpness of model representations can serve as a signal to detect and mitigate hallucinations in large language models. Authors include Shiqi Chen, Miao Xiong, Junteng Liu, Zhengxuan Wu, Teng Xiao, Siyang Gao, and Junxian He.
