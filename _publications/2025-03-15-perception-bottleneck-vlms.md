This paper investigates the perception bottleneck of Vision-Language Models (VLMs) when applied to chart understanding tasks. It analyzes how visual perception limitations in VLMs affect the accuracy of chart-based reasoning, identifying key bottlenecks and directions for improvement. The work is first-authored by Junteng Liu, with co-authors Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He.
