This paper introduces SynLogic, a framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale to learn logical reasoning and other reasoning capabilities. The data generation pipeline produces training examples with verifiable logical steps, enabling models to learn robust reasoning patterns. The work is first-authored by Junteng Liu, with co-authors Yuanxiang Fan, Zhuo Jiang, Han Ding, Yongyi Hu, Chi Zhang, Yiqi Shi, Shitong Weng, Aili Chen, Shiqi Chen, Yunan Huang, Mozhi Zhang, Pengyu Zhao, Junjie Yan, and Junxian He.
